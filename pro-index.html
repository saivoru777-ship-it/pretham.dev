Hi, I'm Pretham.

Right now

I'm a master's student in Applied Machine Learning at the University of Maryland.

I finished a B.S. in Cybersecurity at Purdue in May 2026 and started at Maryland that August. The through-line is the same one I've had since freshman year: I like problems where the answer has to be measured, not asserted.

Before this

I spent fifteen months at Mastercard turning noisy security telemetry into things people could act on.

Fraud signals, correlation and triage workflows, and ML-based threat classification — in a regulated environment where "probably fine" isn't an acceptable answer. Before that, an IT security analyst internship at NSG Info doing log monitoring and vulnerability assessment.

On the side

I ship things. A Chrome extension, a locker platform, a grocery reference, an agent orchestrator.

Some of it is commercial, some of it exists because I wanted it to. The lease analyzer came out of reading my own lease and realizing I couldn't tell which clauses were normal. It's on the Chrome Web Store now.

And then

There's the research, which got out of hand in the best way — thirty-one orders of magnitude.

I built a statistical test for spatial clustering, and then kept finding places it worked: synapses on dendrites, cell types across the mouse brain, protein nanoclusters, and the large-scale structure of the universe. Five manuscripts so far.

Looking for

Backend, infrastructure, applied ML, or security tooling. I'm most useful where correctness matters and failure modes are interesting.
